Teaching and supervision

I teach at postgraduate level at the University of Chester and lead the MSc programme I founded there. Most of my teaching is with international cohorts who arrive without a programming background.

Programme leadership

I am the founding Programme Leader of the MSc in Management of Information Systems and Digital Innovation at Chester. I conceived the programme, designed it, took it through validation, launched it, and lead it.

Current modules

  • CO7062 AI for Modern Use-CasesTaught as SE7048 from 2022 to 2025 and renamed in 2026. The cohort is MSc Electrical and Electronic Engineering students with no prior programming experience, from India, Bangladesh, Nigeria, China, Nepal, Pakistan, Ghana, Vietnam and elsewhere. The module is scaffolded from first-principles Python through to applied AI.
  • CO7608 Blockchain Applications for EnterprisesDelivered as a flipped classroom with smart-contract exercises and group projects. Three peer-reviewed conference papers have come out of supervised group-project work on this module.
  • SE7227 Applied Business Intelligence for Project Managers
  • SE7200 Research DissertationModule Lead, with responsibility for the module as well as supervision.
  • CO7057 MSc EEE ProjectProject supervision. Previously SE7045.
  • Two-year Masters placement projectYear-long project supervision.

Approximate current annual teaching and supervision load: 350 hours. Also taught at Chester: SE7220 Management of Risk, 2022 to 2025, and SE6008 Control Systems Engineering.

Masters supervision

97 Masters dissertations completed and 7 in progress, across MSc Electrical and Electronic Engineering, MSc Project and Programme Management, MSc Management of Information Systems and Digital Innovation, and earlier M.Tech dissertations in India.

Recent dissertation topics cluster on AI agents and human-AI collaboration in project management, explainable AI in automated decision pipelines, governance of AI-driven decision systems in fintech, and large language models for portfolio oversight and compliance.

Also supervised: 32 undergraduate projects, 2 M.Tech projects, and 4 projects through the Institution of Electronics and Telecommunication Engineers. Undergraduate work I supervised produced FireNet, a fire and smoke detection model whose open dataset remains in active use, and an early Devanagari OCR project.
